Viral Structure Research Laboratory

Research Objective

This laboratory simulates experimental data related to viral structure, genome organization, and host-cell infection dynamics. The research visualizes mutation behavior, structural stability, and cellular response during viral replication.

DNA Structure Visualization

DNA helix visualization used to illustrate genome organization and mutation sequencing patterns during viral replication studies.

Infected Cell Observation
